## Metadata
- Author: [[Morgan Levine]]
- Full Title: The Biggest Myth About Aging, According to Science
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Aging is a natural process that happens at the molecular and cellular levels, affecting how healthy we stay over time. Scientists measure aging by looking at biological changes, like DNA methylation, to understand how fast different parts of the body age. While we cannot yet reverse aging, healthy lifestyle choices like diet and fasting can slow it and help us live longer, healthier lives.
