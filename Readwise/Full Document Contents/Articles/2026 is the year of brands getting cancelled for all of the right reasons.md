Marketers ask for time in one language. Finance asks for proof in another. The words never quite meet.

I know we never really got the long end of the stick, but this one hurts because the evidence is not missing. Binet & Field. Ehrenberg-Bass. IPA. Three decades of independent research keep pointing in the same direction: brand builds future demand, improves conversion, protects pricing power, and compounds when it is funded consistently.

This is not a belief system. It is commercial fact, badly translated. And [the cost](https://substack.com/redirect/85e467eb-dc05-45fe-bee9-2eab076828bf?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) is real.

Around 50% of brand’s sales effect can show up in the first four months. The remaining 50% can arrive across months five to twenty-four. [Judge that investment](https://substack.com/redirect/85e467eb-dc05-45fe-bee9-2eab076828bf?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) on the first quarter alone and of course it looks weak. So would planting a tree if you measured it by shade on day ten.

Stop fighting the brand budget battle on vibes alone.
