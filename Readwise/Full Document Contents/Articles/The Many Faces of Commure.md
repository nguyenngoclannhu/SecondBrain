Alright, back to Commure…

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YGpb!,w_300,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0eef0e78-9123-4523-9169-53078b10d217_622x1108.png)](https://substack.com/redirect/e6f95b0e-bd33-4105-899d-d6a843ac04dc?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)While Commure has been failing the medical community, it will never actually fail because it keeps getting propped up by massive cash infusions from General Catalyst and every other VC bro shop Hemant is tied into. Translation: basically the whole Silicon Valley ecosystem.

Many people in healthcare don’t even know what Commure does. Supposedly it “[builds software that makes physicians’ lives easier](https://substack.com/redirect/8080ad61-e1e4-4d2a-99d9-31d7a11b02c3?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)” 🤷‍♂️. But here’s the kicker – it doesn’t matter. No matter what Commure does, the cash will never stop.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!t72-!,w_500,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F428ab0c6-1dc4-4f09-a41e-5231523d81fc_1310x1485.jpeg)](https://substack.com/redirect/b02dfadb-1a2c-4cba-8f73-f517a79badc1?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)Commure might be [the weirdest startup in healthcare](https://substack.com/redirect/b02dfadb-1a2c-4cba-8f73-f517a79badc1?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g), according to [Business Insider](https://substack.com/redirect/b02dfadb-1a2c-4cba-8f73-f517a79badc1?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g). Allegedly, the company did not respond to media questions, and the information in Business Insider’s investigation came primarily from employees. Interestingly, employees were under obligations not to speak to the media but did so anyway because the internal situation was “ridiculous.”

Commure, the so-called $6B “platform” company, has raised approximately $1.014B across 13 rounds, including merger-related equity. (Source: [CBInsights](https://substack.com/redirect/c2b2f7c5-4dfc-4aef-bee7-a6369087882a?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g).) (It is still, amazingly, short of [Ali Parsa and Babylon’s $1.5B total fundraise of someone else’s money that fairly quickly evaporated into dust](https://substack.com/redirect/312a9912-9a01-4902-b2d0-dd0460e4e020?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g).) Commure has cycled through four CEOs in five years and pivoted from its original mission. Yet somehow, it survives. In fact, on paper it even thrives. Why? Because its co-founder and chief backer, Hemant Taneja, has given it a blank check. Taneja has a colossal vision for Commure and, as one former exec put it, “[Taneja will not, under any circumstances, allow Commure to fail](https://substack.com/redirect/b02dfadb-1a2c-4cba-8f73-f517a79badc1?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g).” This sounds to me like Commure is TCF: Too Corrupt to Fail.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!fyjY!,w_1100,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0b21bac1-2198-4055-937e-e2310c4b973a_2036x1092.png)](https://substack.com/redirect/6902a37a-1b88-45d5-92a4-606d183383c1?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)Backed by General Catalyst’s seemingly infinite cash and crony connections, Commure has been Hemant Taneja’s baby. It has become a black hole for investor money and a harbor for shady deals like Memora Health, where, shockingly, General Catalyst was on both sides of the trade in a potential conflict of interest. It is a case study in how hype and hubris can trump reality in healthcare tech.
