I want to share something exciting with you first, before I announce it to my followers on LinkedIn and X.

In 2026, I will be building the [AI by Hand Academy](https://substack.com/redirect/f6b9d69a-1157-424c-931d-75d2e4119e02?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) — a new space where I’ll teach *introductory and foundational AI topics* through structured video-based courses and step-by-step lessons using the AI by Hand method.

The first course to launch in January will be *Introduction to Agentic AI by Hand* ✍️.

I am currently recruiting the first 100 Founding Members. I’d be honored to have you — but there is absolutely no pressure. This newsletter continues to be the place I share my latest AI by Hand worksheets and exercises.

Thank you, always, for being a strong supporter of my effort to teach AI by Hand to the world!

Sincerely,

Prof. Tom Yeh
