- Summary: Visual general intelligence (VGI) is about learning and understanding the world through vision alone, not just combining vision with language. It focuses on building visual systems that can predict, imagine, and act in new and changing environments. The goal is to create vision-based intelligence that adapts, reasons, and works reliably in the real world.
