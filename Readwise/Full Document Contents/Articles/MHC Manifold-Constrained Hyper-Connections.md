- Summary: The paper shows that Hyper-Connections (HC) boost model performance but break residual identity mapping, causing instability and high memory/communication cost. mHC constrains HC onto a manifold to restore identity, improve stability, and keep costs low. With kernel and recomputation optimizations, mHC scales to large models with small overhead and better downstream results.
