##### ChatGPT Health might be a marketing gimmick. But it also looks like a preview of how tech behemoths could undercut the entire digital health market.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0Irt!,w_1100,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4fb0e9ea-ef2b-45af-affa-d1c5012ecee0_800x400.jpeg)](https://substack.com/redirect/b1ab41e8-2aaa-43e3-a8c8-6206c301360a?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)***Welcome to AI Health Uncut, a brutally honest newsletter on AI, innovation, and the state of the healthcare market. If you’d like to sign up to receive issues over email, you can do so [here](https://substack.com/redirect/b67b12b7-4bc2-481d-aac7-64eba6de06f7?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g).***

My readers know that I’ve been preaching AI commoditization, and saying it’s a matter of time before tech giants come in and take over entire sectors of healthcare. Right now, the margins are still super thin for them. So they don’t really care. But they are testing the waters.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9oWX!,w_400,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8fb710d5-1263-40ec-aeaa-56e1e9cbcc8f_1487x1426.jpeg)](https://substack.com/redirect/2fc80767-fc91-43e5-9da3-5f331226734b?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)[ChatGPT Health, which OpenAI announced yesterday (January 7, 2026)](https://substack.com/redirect/2fc80767-fc91-43e5-9da3-5f331226734b?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g), may be a step in that direction. ChatGPT Health is basically a ChatGPT wrapper on a dedicated server, with claims of “enhanced security” and a promise that no OpenAI model will be trained on patient data.

OMG. So many questions.

OK. Back to ChatGPT Health…

So many questions following [the ChatGPT Health announcement](https://substack.com/redirect/2fc80767-fc91-43e5-9da3-5f331226734b?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g):

* “Enhanced security.” Soooo, what kind of security does everyday ChatGPT have then? “Less than enhanced?” What does that even mean?
* You promise not to train your models. But can you still fine-tune your models?
* [23andMe also promised a lot of things about the security of customer DNA data](https://substack.com/redirect/30532d1e-6f00-4c52-85a6-d186f6a70c7b?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g). And then one day they sent an unassuming email titled “Change to Terms & Conditions” that, of course, no one ever opens. And voilà, they can sell whatever they want to third parties (as we found out the hard way during the 23andMe bankruptcy filing last year).

I predict we’re going to forget about ChatGPT Health in a few weeks, just like we quickly forgot **other OpenAI awkward crusades into healthcare**:

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!lEAR!,w_1100,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1e90ec8a-558a-40cf-8ded-6bf73a7c7185_1660x758.png)](https://substack.com/redirect/c74c3a26-d291-40af-99d8-c8ca8661994f?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)* Remember [OpenAI’s HealthBench](https://substack.com/redirect/6f40a7fe-6835-4e61-bec1-3564109b8958?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)? A carefully designed and curated dataset built on just 5,000 medical conversations. Not the model. The data. Just prompting, and of course the claim that OpenAI’s models did so well on their own benchmark. Who would have guessed. 🙂

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mxNC!,w_1100,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F94b87801-b48c-46da-bd19-83bee52032e4_2258x1258.png)](https://substack.com/redirect/12894419-eac4-4cfc-b5de-d65568fa5214?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)* Remember how [OpenAI shamefully used a cancer survivor](https://substack.com/redirect/12894419-eac4-4cfc-b5de-d65568fa5214?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) to promote the health-related features of GPT-5, only [to cut those features back less than three months later after numerous suicide-related lawsuits](https://substack.com/redirect/7443354a-ff4d-4dd3-a0a5-a56970cc7184?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)?

Of course, we don’t remember any of these. And honestly, in the case of GPT-5, I’m pretty sure OpenAI wants you to forget everything. Because they are marketing gimmicks, designed to convince the medical community that it’s OK to use OpenAI’s gadgets. No new technology was introduced. All of these gimmicks were basically wrappers on ChatGPT.

And I think the story will continue with ChatGPT Health.
