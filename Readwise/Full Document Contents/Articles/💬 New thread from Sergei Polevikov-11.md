Sergei Polevikov I couldn’t believe my eyes when I saw this…

I want to ask you, my paid subscribers and Founding members, about a phenomenon I’ve noticed in 2025. People like Hemant Taneja and Marc Andreessen keep telling us how incredible AI progress in healthcare has been. But outside of a few secretive private “pump and dump” deals at crazy valuations that no sane person can rationally justify, the reality has been pretty gloomy for AI and data analytics in 2025. That is based on public records, financial metrics, and my conversations with clinicians, including while moderating the Health2Tech panel on December 4.

I couldn’t believe my eyes when I saw the real numbers. I’m going to report on all of this in the year end review on my Substack tomorrow morning.
