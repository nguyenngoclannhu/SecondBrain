Former President Bill Clinton and his wife, former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton, were ordered to appear before Congress for depositions on the Jeffrey Epstein probe next month after the two powerful Democrats attempted to avoid the House Oversight Committee’s grilling.

The Clintons’ lawyer wrote a letter to the House Oversight Committee on November 3 in an attempt to get the former president and former Secretary of State out of testifying in person. The Clintons’ attorney asked if they could simply submit “a written proffer of what little information” they have on Epstein. House Oversight Committee Chairman James Comer (R-KY) [denied](https://x.com/RepJamesComer/status/1991877484208595253) that request on Friday and ordered the Clintons to appear for their depositions on December 17 and 18.

“As your letter indicates, both President Clinton and Secretary Clinton possess information relevant to the Committee’s investigation,” Comer wrote to the Clintons’ attorney, David Kendall.

“Additionally, your suggestion that your clients’ testimony would not be relevant to the stated purposes of the Committee’s investigation because the events in question took place outside of the Clintons’ respective official duties, misses the Committee’s point,” Comer added. “It is precisely the fact President Clinton and Secretary Clinton each maintained relationships with Mr. Epstein and Ms. [Ghislaine] Maxwell in their personal capacities as private citizens that is of interest to the Committee.”

Former President Clinton is scheduled to appear before the committee at 10:00 a.m. on December 17, while the former first lady is set to appear at 1o:00 a.m. on December 18.

A House Oversight panel [voted](https://www.dailywire.com/news/house-panel-votes-to-subpoena-epstein-files-clintons-ex-law-enforcement-leaders) in July to subpoena the Clintons, along with former law enforcement leaders and more files on convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein. Three Republicans joined with five Democrats in the vote to approve the subpoenas. Comer then issued the subpoenas for the Clintons on August 5.

The Clintons were ordered to appear for their depositions after President Donald Trump [directed](https://www.dailywire.com/news/trump-unleashes-doj-to-investigate-epsteins-democrat-friends) the Justice Department to investigate former President Clinton over his ties to Epstein. Attorney General Pam Bondi [said](https://www.dailywire.com/news/bondi-says-new-info-warrants-more-epstein-investigations-as-doj-sets-sights-on-his-dem-pals?topStoryPosition=undefined&author=Zach+Jewell&category=News&elementPosition=0&row=1&rowHeadline=Top+Stories&rowType=Top+Stories&title=Bondi+Says+%E2%80%98New+Info%E2%80%99+Warrants+More+Epstein+Investigations+As+DOJ+Sets+Sights+On+His+Dem+Pals) on Wednesday that “new information” warrants further investigations into Epstein and those connected to him after the Justice Department closed its investigation into Epstein earlier this year.

Trump also signed legislation earlier this week to release the remaining Epstein files after arguing that Democrats have focused on what he has called the Epstein “hoax” to deflect from other issues.

Bill Clinton traveled with Epstein and Maxwell multiple times, with flight records indicating that the former president was with Epstein on his infamous “Lolita Express,” the airplane Epstein is accused of using to sex traffic underage girls. The former president [traveled](https://abcnews.go.com/Politics/bill-clinton-jeffrey-epstein/story?id=127629827) with the financier and his associate to numerous countries, including Bangkok, Brunei, Rwanda, Russia, and China.

Trump has suggested that Bill Clinton visited Epstein’s island in the Virgin Islands, but the former president has denied that he ever set foot on the island. In an email released by the House Oversight Committee earlier this month, Epstein wrote that Bill Clinton “was NEVER EVER there, never.”

There is no evidence that Hillary traveled with Epstein, and neither Bill nor Hillary has been accused of any wrongdoing related to Epstein’s sex crimes.

The Clintons’ attorney stated in his November 3 letter that both Bill and Hillary “welcome legitimate oversight,” seeking to uncover the truth behind Epstein’s and Maxwell’s crimes. The attorney added that the Clintons “have little to contribute to that legitimate goal, all of which can be readily submitted on paper.”

Comer argued that the committee is “entitled to a fulsome examination” of the information that the Clintons possess on Epstein and “the ability to elicit the information in person and to ask relevant follow-up questions.”
