Happy New Year!

What’s your New Year’s resolution? Learning AI every week?

Me too. For me, the best way to learn is to teach. Every time I teach a subject, I learn something new.

So, as my resolution for 2026, I’m committing to teaching one live AI seminar every week, by hand ✍️.

If building a regular AI learning habit is on your list too, I’d love for you to join me for the very first seminar.
