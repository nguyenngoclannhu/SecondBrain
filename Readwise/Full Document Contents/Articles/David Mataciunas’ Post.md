CTO & Co-Founder @ AQ22 | AI Agents for financial analysis, credit assessment and beyond

📌 Here’s my go-to list of AI blogs that help you learn something new:

1️⃣ [Chip Huyen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chiphuyen?trk=public_post-text) – Practical insights on ML systems, production AI, and scaling AI in real-world applications.

2️⃣ [Eugene Yan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eugeneyan?trk=public_post-text) – Covers practical ML, data science, and building AI systems that actually work in production.

3️⃣ Lil’Log by [Lilian Weng](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lilianweng?trk=public_post-text) – Deep dives into AI/ML research, reinforcement learning, and neural networks.

4️⃣ [Sebastian Raschka, PhD](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sebastianraschka?trk=public_post-text) – A mix of AI research, ML engineering, and Python implementations explained in depth.

5️⃣ [Simon Willison](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simonwillison?trk=public_post-text) – Focuses on LLMs, AI tooling, and making AI more accessible for developers.

6️⃣ Andrej Karpathy – One of the best minds in deep learning, with highly technical and philosophical takes on AI.

7️⃣ [Nathan Lambert](https://www.linkedin.com/in/natolambert?trk=public_post-text) ([Interconnects AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/interconnects-ai?trk=public_post-text)) – Covers the latest AI research mainly focusing on Reinforcement Learning.

8️⃣ [Ethan Mollick](https://www.linkedin.com/in/emollick?trk=public_post-text) – AI’s impact on business, creativity, and society, with an engaging writing style.

9️⃣ Gwern – In-depth essays on AI, rationality, and long-term AI implications.

🔟 [Sebastian Ruder](https://de.linkedin.com/in/sebastianruder?trk=public_post-text) – One of the best blogs for NLP, multilingual AI, and AI research summaries.

Must-Follow AI Company Blogs  

🔹 [Hugging Face](https://www.linkedin.com/company/huggingface?trk=public_post-text) – The latest in open-source AI, LLMs, and AI model releases.  

🔹 [Anthropic](https://www.linkedin.com/company/anthropicresearch?trk=public_post-text) – Safety, interpretability, and responsible AI research.  

🔹 [Sakana AI](https://jp.linkedin.com/company/sakana-ai?trk=public_post-text) – A fresh take on AI model design and innovation.  

🔹 [Sequoia Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sequoia?trk=public_post-text) – AI trends and startup insights from a VC perspective.  

🔹 a16z – AI, technology, and business trends from [Andreessen Horowitz](https://www.linkedin.com/company/a16z?trk=public_post-text).  

🔹 [Google DeepMind](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/googledeepmind?trk=public_post-text) – Cutting-edge AI research and breakthroughs.  

🔹 [AI at Meta](https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/aiatmeta/?trk=public_post-text) – LLMs, AI advances, and Meta’s AI research.

🔥 These blogs cut through the noise and provide real value. Which ones do you follow? Any great AI blogs I should add?
