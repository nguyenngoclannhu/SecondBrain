### **HLTH in Las Vegas**

So I came to [HLTH](https://substack.com/redirect/8e1b8ab6-d712-4acf-a281-0beee017a85f?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) as an influencer 🙂 Which basically meant doing a lot of “influencing” with my good friend [Alex Koshykov](https://substack.com/redirect/68a7dfc7-5d17-4a4c-8017-2e02d16ec2d8?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) — mostly for our podcast *[Digital Health Inside Out](https://substack.com/redirect/9e76f068-7da1-4fe2-935a-62ada36aadc1?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)*. The HLTH episode drops soon, so stay tuned.

For me, the real action wasn’t on stage. It was in the halls, lounges, and late-night meetups — reconnecting with old friends and making new ones. That’s what HLTH is really about.

I glanced at a few panels, and honestly, they were disappointing. Panelists were throwing out claims left and right with zero pushback. Moderators just nodded along, glued to their scripts, afraid to challenge anyone. **What’s the fcking point?**

### **One of my strangest encounters at HLTH**

But the most interesting (and honestly, the weirdest) interaction happened in the exhibit hall, where I spotted the Infermedica booth. I saw this awesome-looking unicorn (like the one in the photo) and immediately thought, “I have to get it.” Not for me, of course, but for a kid I know. Or was it really for the kid? 😉

The woman at the booth said it was the last one they had, but after a little bit of pleading, she smiled and handed it to me. 🙂 I told her I’d been following Infermedica for quite a while. She pointed to someone nearby and said, “Oh, that’s our CEO, Piotr.”

I said, “I know Piotr, of course.” That’s [Piotr Orzechowski](https://substack.com/redirect/1880f01a-0f51-493c-8466-a8f23d8f403a?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g)—the legend of digital health in Eastern Europe—not to be confused with the jazz pianist of the same name. My first thought was, “What’s a CEO of a $50–100M ARR company doing working his own booth late in the afternoon in Las Vegas? Doesn’t he have people for that?”

I introduced myself and my friend [Alex Koshykov](https://substack.com/redirect/68a7dfc7-5d17-4a4c-8017-2e02d16ec2d8?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g). “You probably don’t know me,” I said, “but I’ve been following your company as a researcher.” He looked at my badge and said, “Of course I know you. I’ve been following your Substack. You’ve been critical of my company.” Then he quoted, word for word, a line from my article last year—the one where I tore into [Infermedica’s 97% accuracy claim](https://substack.com/redirect/801e8bbe-43b9-44ab-b558-5e4a1427c72a?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) (and yes, I still stand by that analysis).

I was stunned. I told him, “If you disagreed with my analysis, you should’ve reached out.” Piotr replied that he respects my journalistic integrity and doesn’t want to interfere, even when it’s about his own company. I liked that answer a lot. I also reminded him that in that same piece, I mentioned I’m a big fan and hold him in the highest regard — as an entrepreneur who built one of the strongest sales teams in the digital health industry, starting from humble beginnings in Wrocław, Poland.

I think so.

### **Health AI Summit in Albuquerque**

What [an incredible, high-caliber conference](https://substack.com/redirect/a1f8f96c-8a71-4d0e-9dbc-dbd1a416c1bd?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g). I was truly honored to be invited as a speaker. It took five(!) flight connections in one day(!) just to get there. But I’m so glad I did. [Stefany Goradia](https://substack.com/redirect/676a4ce0-1676-46bc-b039-b1c63c078c7a?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) and [Angelica Maestas](https://substack.com/redirect/5971e79e-08f9-446c-8d4a-113f5bcfe970?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) pulled off something extraordinary, bringing together 240 healthcare AI experts in one room. I’m still speechless, and honestly can’t fathom how they managed to do it.

I was blown away by the innovators and by the medical community across the state who spent the day pitching ideas and exchanging honest perspectives.

* [Emily Maginn](https://substack.com/redirect/d79a5b9b-a4bb-4652-8ed8-bd7965744b48?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) spoke about her groundbreaking work in women’s health with Exo—a unified data system that begins collecting data right at the point of care. It’s about time we built a dataset that isn’t centered around white men in their 30s and 40s but represents real women’s health.
* [Amit Mehta](https://substack.com/redirect/85e43c05-2d87-4fe3-a215-44d486cd9d68?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g), CEO of Amplifier Health, shared their voice-based diagnostic system, where models are trained on audio sequences rather than text. Tokens aren’t words—they’re sound patterns. Now that’s what *not* being an AI tourist looks like. 😉
* [Galina Priloutskaya](https://substack.com/redirect/d995b23c-37c0-427e-823a-74b285d41f09?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI_zv_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g) from HealthInsight showcased a remarkable project using technology to bring nutritious food to Indian reservations, building trust with the Indian Health Services (IHS) in New Mexico and the Dakotas. It’s inspiring to see AI and technology directly improving the lives of minority and underserved populations.

<p style="margin: 0 0 20px 0;color: rgb(54,55,55);line-height: 26px;font-size: 16px;">But the most interesting (and honestly, the weirdest) interaction happened in the exhibit hall, where I spotted the Infermedica booth. I saw this awesome-looking unicorn (like the one in the photo) and immediately thought, “I have to get it.” Not for me, of course, but for a kid I know. Or was it really for the kid? 😉</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 20px 0;color: rgb(54,55,55);line-height: 26px;font-size: 16px;">The woman at the booth said it was the last one they had, but after a little bit of pleading, she smiled and handed it to me. 🙂 I told her I’d been following Infermedica for quite a while. She pointed to someone nearby and said, “Oh, that’s our CEO, Piotr.”</p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 20px 0;color: rgb(54,55,55);line-height: 26px;font-size: 16px;"><span>I said, “I know Piotr, of course.” That’s </span><a href="https://substack.com/redirect/1880f01a-0f51-493c-8466-a8f23d8f403a?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI\_zv\_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g" rel="" style="color: rgb(54,55,55);text-decoration: underline;">Piotr Orzechowski</a><span>—the legend of digital health in Eastern Europe—not to be confused with the jazz pianist of the same name. My first thought was, “What’s a CEO of a $50–100M ARR company doing working his own booth late in the afternoon in Las Vegas? Doesn’t he have people for that?”</span></p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 20px 0;color: rgb(54,55,55);line-height: 26px;font-size: 16px;"><span>I introduced myself and my friend </span><a href="https://substack.com/redirect/68a7dfc7-5d17-4a4c-8017-2e02d16ec2d8?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI\_zv\_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g" rel="" style="color: rgb(54,55,55);text-decoration: underline;">Alex Koshykov</a><span>. “You probably don’t know me,” I said, “but I’ve been following your company as a researcher.” He looked at my badge and said, “Of course I know you. I’ve been following your Substack. You’ve been critical of my company.” Then he quoted, word for word, a line from my article last year—the one where I tore into </span><a href="https://substack.com/redirect/801e8bbe-43b9-44ab-b558-5e4a1427c72a?j=eyJ1IjoiNnE2ZWdsIn0.JeUaI03hkTZe3g0Y-WbKcpI\_zv\_M4N2A50pyRXMoP1g" rel="" style="color: rgb(54,55,55);text-decoration: underline;">Infermedica’s 97% accuracy claim</a><span> (and yes, I still stand by that analysis).</span></p>
<p style="margin: 0 0 20px 0;color: rgb(54,55,55);line-height: 26px;font-size: 16px;">I was stunned. I told him, “If you disagreed with my analysis, you should’ve reached out.” Piotr replied that he respects my journalistic integrity and doesn’t want to interfere, even when it’s about his own company. I liked that answer a lot. I also reminded him that in that same piece, I mentioned I’m a big fan and hold him in the highest regard — as an entrepreneur who built one of the strongest sales teams in the digital health industry, starting from humble beginnings in Wrocław, Poland.</p>
