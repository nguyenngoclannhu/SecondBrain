Sergei Polevikov 🚨 Dear friends: As I'm in the middle of multiple healthcare AI investigations right now (Commure, Function Health, Mayo Clinic, Fruit Street Health, OpenEvidence, Care.ai, Andor Health, Nucleus Genomics) - if you have any info on any of these, please share. Everything stays strictly confidential. 

I also have an announcement.

🎥 I don’t know if my subscribers, many of whom I’m honored to call my friends, need any Thanksgiving presents. But here it is. Alex Koshykov and I just launched a new YouTube series, "Digital Health Vitals," together with two other musketeers of digital health, Ben Schwartz, MD, MBA, and Rik Renard, RN. 🩺

If you like my Substack, I think you’re going to love this podcast.

🔥 In Episode 1 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ov69KPZLOkg), we dug into what we thought were the hottest topics of the month.

1. Hippocratic AI raised $126M ($404M total) without any real progress in technology. Is there actually a future for AI agents in healthcare 🤖❓ 
2. A recent Nature article shows that while GPT-5 has made progress reducing hallucinations, it still fails in more than half of difficult clinical scenarios. Is there any use for LLMs in medicine beyond AI scribes? 🧠📉 https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-04008-8
3. Suicides by ChatGPT. OpenAI published a report saying they improved ChatGPT's mental-health responses with help from 170 experts and cut "undesired responses" by 65–80%. But is this real progress, or just patching things up after multiple suicides linked to ChatGPT?
4. CMS is at it again. CY-2026 Physician Fee Schedule and the –2.5% efficiency adjustment. When will this sh\*t end? 😒
5. Epic cuts ties with Abridge. Is it Abridge's loss? Don't be so sure. 👀

🔗 Episode 1 is live: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ov69KPZLOkg

Happy Thanksgiving, everyone! 🦃 I hope you can get off social media and spend time with your family and friends. ❤️
