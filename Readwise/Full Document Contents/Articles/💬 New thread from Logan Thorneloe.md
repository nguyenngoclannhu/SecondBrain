Logan Thorneloe Here's what I'm thinking for ML for SWEs going forward:

> We're going to explore a machine learning system about once a week. This means I'll pick something on Sunday and by Friday I'm going to have an article + some sort of hands-on practicum. This makes it perfect to just read if want or a nice weekend project.

> Roundups will continue but I'd like to go deeper on less things. Instead of being fully comprehensive about everything that happened really contextualize the 4-5 most important events. Let me know what you think about this!

> Career development will remain for paid subs. I think there's a TON we can do here. We've got a great community with a bunch of talented engineers. I want to connect talent with opportunities and provide discounts on learning materials, AI dev tools, etc.

I'd love to hear your thoughts!
