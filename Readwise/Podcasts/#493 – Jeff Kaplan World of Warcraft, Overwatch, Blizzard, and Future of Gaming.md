- **Why Carmack Changed Gaming Beyond Graphics**
  - Jeff sees John Carmack and id Software as foundational because they advanced not just graphics but smoothness, responsiveness, and feel.
  - Wolfenstein 3D and Doom crossed the realism threshold where players felt transported into a world.

You'd run around, you'd dress up as a German, you'd throw a grenade. To see it in 3D.
