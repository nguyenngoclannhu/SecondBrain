But who's creating it and who is it being created for? So you're simultaneously the creator and the watcher, which is kind of amazing. I'm creating a simulated experience for myself. - **Decoding Whale Language**
  - James Cameron highlights a marine biology project led by David Gruber that uses AI and machine learning to decode whale vocalizations.
  - Researchers are analyzing thousands of hours of sperm whale vocalizations, paired with contextual footage, to understand their complex communication.
  - They've discovered that whales use clicks (codas) in sequences with verbs and syntax, suggesting a language as complex as human language.
  - Cameron states that nature is far more complex than we realize, and consciousness is shared by higher mammals and even some birds.
  - Recognition in a mirror is a key indicator of higher consciousness, a trait observed in elephants, chimpanzees and dolphins.
  - Studying great whales is challenging because of their size.
  Transcript:
  James Cameron
  We won't protect what we don't love and care about, right? And so I'm working on a very small part of a much bigger project that's being run by a marine biologist named David Gruber. So they've got thousands of hours of sperm whale vocalizations, and they've got some context footage of what socially they're doing, and they're decoding their clicks, which are Called codas, and their click sequences.
