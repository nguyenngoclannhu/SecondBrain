## Highlights
- Disrupt Habits with Opposing Narratives
  Habit disruption requires injecting novelty into routines, as the nervous system responds to new and different stimuli. Simply replacing familiar concepts with their opposites can serve as a significant disruptor to automatic behaviors. This approach is more effective than traditional methods like rubber bands or sticky notes, which often lack sufficient impact due to familiarity. The brain seeks efficiency, so introducing an alternative narrative becomes a powerful tool in altering ingrained habits by prompting awareness and reflection.
