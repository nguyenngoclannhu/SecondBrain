- **Citizen Killed During ICE Operation**
  - Renee Good, a 37-year US citizen, was shot and killed by an ICE agent while observing an ICE arrest operation. 
  - Videos show ICE officers near her car and one officer firing three shots as she appeared to turn the wheel to drive away.
  Transcript:
  Casey Magloire
  Last week, Renee Good, a 37-year US citizen and activist, was shot and killed in her car by an ICE agent in Minneapolis. Renee Good had driven to a neighbourhood to witness an ICE arrest operation. Videos recorded on the scene show ICE officers walking around her car. One of them fires three shots directly at her as she's seemingly turning the steering wheel to drive away from him. Donald Trump justified the killing as self-defence, claiming that Renee Good had tried to run the officer over. In the aftermath of the killing, thousands of people took to the streets of cities such as Boston, Portland, San Francisco and Minneapolis, calling for an end to ICE's mass detentions. Just a week after Renee Good's death, another man was shot in the leg in Minneapolis by an ICE agent. The incident sparked new protests and fresh clashes with law enforcement.
