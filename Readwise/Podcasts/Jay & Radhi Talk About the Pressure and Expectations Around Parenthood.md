- **Assumptions Behind 'When Are You Having Kids?'**
  - The question 'When are you having kids?' often skips the more fundamental question of whether the person even wants children.
  - This question presumes everyone can conceive and have children, ignoring potential struggles with fertility.
  - It is often insensitive and unthoughtful, despite usually stemming from good intentions like excitement or care.
  - Jay uses an analogy: It's similar to asking someone when they'll start a business when they're content with their job.

- **Preparing for Parenthood**
  - Instead of focusing on 'when' to have kids, prepare for the significant life changes that come with parenthood. 
  - Consider how your sleep, social life, and relationship with your partner may change.
  - Acknowledge that you may become a lower priority, and prepare for sleepless nights and less freedom.
  - Shift your focus from timing to understanding and embracing the inevitable changes.
