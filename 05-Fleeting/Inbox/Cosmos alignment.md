- In "The Alchemist" there is a story of a boy who ask the great minds how to achieve happiness. The great minds ask him to go through the house while holding a spoon full of oil. Then the man walks through the house full of spectacular stuffs but focus his eyes on the spoon. The Great Mind ask him how was the house and he failed to notice anything since he was so focused on the spoon. The man then allowed to walk the house one more time with the spoon. This time the man has successfully name all the stuff at the house but failed to keep the oil spoon full. He said that achieved both things at the same time is the way to achieve happiness. 
- Giống như khi ta đi kinh hành, cả 2 việc niệm kinh và đi đúng bước chân đều phải được thực hiện. Chỉ khi cả 2 đều đạt được thì ta sẽ có được sự bình yên mà tiếng niệm kinh đem lại.
